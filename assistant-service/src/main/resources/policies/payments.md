# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard and RuPay) and net banking. We do not offer cash on delivery.

## When you are charged
Your payment is taken while the order is PENDING, after its stock has been reserved. If the payment fails, the order is CANCELLED and the stock is released. If an order is cancelled after you have paid, the full amount is refunded automatically.

## Card security
Card details are handled by our payment provider. EcomDemo never sees or stores your card number.
