# Warranty

## Electronics
Every electronic product - keyboards, mice, webcams, headphones, USB hubs and storage - has a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects, from the date of delivery.

## Displays
Monitors have a 3-year warranty, which also covers dead pixels on the panel.

## Accessories
Accessories with no electronics, such as laptop stands, desk mats and laptop sleeves, have a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects.

## What the warranty does not cover
Accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear and tear, and damage from unauthorised repairs are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact customer support with your order number and a description of the fault. We repair or replace the product within 10 business days of receiving it.
