# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return a product within 30 days of delivery if it is unused, in its original packaging, and has all its accessories.

## Products that cannot be returned
Storage devices such as SSDs cannot be returned once their seal has been broken, to protect everyone's data. Products damaged by misuse cannot be returned.

## How to return a product
Contact customer support with your order number. Pickup is free, and we collect the product within 3 business days.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product passes inspection. Shipping charges are refunded only if the product was faulty or wrong.

## Damaged or wrong products
If a product arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, tell customer support within 48 hours of delivery, with photos. We replace it free of charge, or refund it in full if no replacement is in stock.
