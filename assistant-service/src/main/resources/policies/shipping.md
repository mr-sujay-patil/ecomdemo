# Shipping and delivery

## Where we deliver
We deliver anywhere in India. We do not ship outside India.

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day are dispatched the same day. Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days to metro cities and 4 to 7 business days to the rest of India.

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free on orders of 5,000 rupees or more. Orders below 5,000 rupees pay a flat 99 rupees for standard shipping. Express delivery in 1 to 2 business days is available to metro cities only, for 249 rupees on any order.

## Tracking an order
When an order is dispatched we email a tracking link. An order is dispatched only after it is CONFIRMED; a PENDING order is still waiting for its stock and payment to be settled.
